Help

What is this?

A local web app that connects to your Rheem EcoNet heat pump water heater through the same cloud backend the official EcoNet phone app uses. It gives you more granular usage tracking, TOU cost breakdowns for Seattle City Light rates, and schedule optimization to save money.

Your data stays on your computer. The only external communication is with Rheem's ClearBlade servers (the same ones the phone app talks to).

Dashboard

At-a-glance view of your water heater. Shows hot water availability, setpoint, operating mode, and running status.

  • Setpoint +/− — adjusts target temperature via MQTT (110–140°F)
  • Mode popover — click to open, select a mode. Changes push to device instantly via MQTT.
  • Energy chart — hourly bars colored by TOU rate tier
  • Refresh button — forces an immediate poll from EcoNet cloud
Schedule

Set up to 4 temperature/mode changes per day, per day of the week.

  • Timeline — colored blocks across 24 hours. Click to edit. Toggle TOU overlay to see rate windows.
  • List — table view with time, setpoint, mode.
  • Week — all 7 days stacked. Click a day to edit it.
  • Copy to… — copy one day's schedule to other days.
  • Optimizer — analyzes TOU rates and suggests a cost-saving schedule with estimated monthly/yearly savings.
History

Historical energy consumption and heating cycles.

  • Hourly chart — today/yesterday kWh per hour, colored by TOU tier
  • Daily chart — stacked bars (peak/mid/off-peak) with cost line overlay
  • Export — download CSV at hourly or daily granularity, up to 2 years

Note: Energy data for Gen5 firmware is tracked from heater state snapshots (running state over time), not from a dedicated energy API. The EcoNet REST energy endpoints are not available for this firmware version.

Settings
  • Device card — shows location, model, firmware, MQTT status, serial/MAC, health
  • EcoNet Account — same credentials as your phone app. Show/hide password supported.
  • Comfort Preferences — preferred setpoint, minimum temp, max recovery time (used by optimizer)
  • TOU heatmap — visual 7-day × 24-hour grid. Click "Edit Rates" to customize tiers.
  • Debug tools — expand the drawer to inspect MQTT status, recent messages, raw device data

How it connects

Communication uses MQTT (real-time messaging) to Rheem's ClearBlade IoT platform — the same backend the EcoNet phone app uses. The connection icon in the nav bar shows:

  • Green link — connected. Hover for device summary.
  • Red — disconnected. Click to go to Settings.

Mode changes and setpoint adjustments are sent via MQTT and apply within seconds. State is also polled every 5 minutes as a fallback. The REST energy/schedule APIs are not available for Gen5 firmware — all data flows through MQTT.
