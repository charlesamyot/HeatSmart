Help

What is WattWise?

WattWise is a whole-home energy savings platform that monitors and optimizes your connected appliances using time-of-use (TOU) electricity rates. It connects to device providers like Rheem EcoNet to give you real-time control, detailed energy tracking, and smart scheduling that shifts usage to cheaper off-peak hours.

Key capabilities:

  • Multi-device support — manage water heaters, HVAC, EV chargers, and more from one dashboard
  • Location management — organize devices across multiple homes or properties
  • TOU rate service — look up your utility's rate plan by ZIP code or browse by region
  • Savings optimizer — AI-generated schedules that minimize energy costs
  • Supabase authentication — secure sign-in with email/password, magic link, or password reset

Your data stays on your computer by default. Cloud sync via Supabase is optional.

Devices

WattWise supports multiple devices and device types. Navigate to the Devices page to manage your connected appliances.

  • Add Device — choose a provider (EcoNet, SmartThings, or Manual), enter credentials, and WattWise will discover and register your equipment
  • Device types — water heater, dishwasher, washer, dryer, EV charger, HVAC, and other
  • Location assignment — assign each device to a location using the dropdown on the device card
  • Device detail — click a device to see its full status, credentials, firmware, and comfort preferences
  • Live status — for EcoNet devices, see real-time temperature, mode, and running state via MQTT
Locations

Organize your devices by physical location. Each location can have its own address, utility provider, and rate plan.

  • Add a location — go to Settings, click "+ Add Location", fill in name and address
  • Primary location — one location is marked as primary (shown with a star). The sidebar selector lets you switch between locations.
  • Utility auto-detection — when you enter a ZIP code, WattWise tries to match your local utility provider
  • Assign devices — on the Devices page, use the location dropdown on each device card to assign it
Dashboard

At-a-glance view of your primary device. Shows hot water availability, setpoint, operating mode, and running status.

  • Setpoint +/- — adjusts target temperature via MQTT (110-140 degrees F)
  • Mode popover — click to open, select a mode. Changes push to device instantly via MQTT.
  • Energy chart — hourly bars colored by TOU rate tier
  • Refresh button — forces an immediate poll from EcoNet cloud
Schedule & Optimizer

Set up to 4 temperature/mode changes per day, per day of the week.

  • Timeline — colored blocks across 24 hours. Click to edit. Toggle TOU overlay to see rate windows.
  • List — table view with time, setpoint, mode.
  • Week — all 7 days stacked. Click a day to edit it.
  • Copy to... — copy one day's schedule to other days.
  • Optimizer — analyzes your TOU rates and water usage patterns, then generates a cost-saving schedule with estimated monthly and yearly savings.
History & Export

Historical energy consumption data with TOU cost breakdown.

  • Hourly chart — today/yesterday kWh per hour, colored by TOU tier
  • Daily chart — stacked bars (peak/mid/off-peak) with cost line overlay
  • Export — download CSV at hourly or daily granularity, up to 2 years
TOU Rates

WattWise uses your utility's time-of-use rate schedule to calculate energy costs and optimize device scheduling.

  • Find Utility Rates — on the Settings page, click "Find Utility Rates" to search by ZIP code or browse Country > Region > Utility > Rate Plan
  • Apply a plan — preview rate blocks and click "Apply This Rate Plan" to set them as your local rates
  • Edit Manually — click "Edit Manually" to add/modify rate tiers directly
  • Heatmap — the 7-day x 24-hour grid shows which tier applies at every hour of the week

Default rates are Seattle City Light TOU. The optimizer pre-heats during off-peak hours (often 50% cheaper than peak) and coasts on stored heat during peak.

Authentication & Accounts

WattWise uses Supabase Auth for user accounts. You can sign in with:

  • Email + Password — create an account on the signup page
  • Magic Link — enter your email and receive a one-click sign-in link
  • Password Reset — click "Forgot password?" on the login page to receive a reset link

Your session token is stored locally and refreshes automatically. The account menu in the sidebar shows your login status and lets you sign out.

Settings
  • Locations — add, edit, and manage your physical locations
  • Account — view your email, set display name, sign out
  • App Status — EcoNet connection status, MQTT live/polling indicator, last poll time
  • TOU Rate Schedule — visual heatmap, utility rate lookup, manual editor
  • Debug tools — expand the drawer to inspect MQTT status, recent messages, raw device data

How it connects

For EcoNet devices, communication uses MQTT (real-time messaging) to Rheem's ClearBlade IoT platform. The connection icon in the sidebar shows:

  • Green link -- connected. Hover for device summary.
  • Red -- disconnected. Click to go to Settings.

Mode changes and setpoint adjustments are sent via MQTT and apply within seconds. State is also polled every 5 minutes as a fallback.

Device Safety

WattWise allows remote control of your water heater, including temperature adjustments and mode changes. Improper settings may result in scalding hot water, Legionella bacteria growth (below 120 degrees F), equipment damage, or increased energy costs. You are solely responsible for ensuring safe operation of your water heater. Always follow your manufacturer's safety guidelines.

WattWise stores your EcoNet credentials in encrypted form (AES-256) to connect to your account on your behalf. While we employ industry-standard encryption, no system is completely secure.
